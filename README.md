Milestone 2 – Accessibility and Visual Design

Accessibility

I worked on making my website easier for people to use. I added labels to my contact form and made sure my pages had headings that were easy to understand.

Visual Design

I used two Gestalt principles called proximity and similarity.

I used proximity by keeping my food cards and information together so everything looks organized.

I used similarity by using the same colors, fonts, and styles on all three pages.

Contact Form

I added a contact form where people can type their name, email, and message. I also added labels and required fields so people know what information they need to enter.
